3. Gaussian Elimination by Hand

This check matches items 2 and 3 on pages 3 and 4. The notes show these steps for reference. The course uses Desmos to solve.

Check 7 (Gaussian Elimination). In [1 3 | 8; 2 −5 | 5]matrix with row 1: 1, 3, bar, 8; row 2: 2, negative 5, bar, 5, which operation makes the 2 below the leading 1 become 0?
Show answer

Multiply row 1 by −2 and add it to row 2.
