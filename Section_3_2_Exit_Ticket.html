3. Zeros and Multiplicity

These checks match item 3 on pages 1 and 2 and Classroom Examples 3.2.5 and 3.2.6 on page 4.

Check 5 (Even and Odd Multiplicity). A zero of a polynomial has multiplicity 2. What does the graph do there?
Show answer

It touches the x-axis and turns around.

Where this is in your notes: item 3 (Multiplicity of a Polynomial Function), parts a and b, on page 1 of the notes.

Check 6 (Classroom Example 3.2.5). For f(x) = 3(x − 2)(x + 5)²f of x equals 3 times the quantity x minus 2, times the quantity x plus 5 squared, which statement is correct?
Show answer

x = 2 has multiplicity 1 and crosses. x = −5 has multiplicity 2 and touches and turns.

Where this is in your notes: item 3 on pages 1 and 2, Classroom Example 3.2.5 on page 4 of the notes and the video "Classroom Example 3.2.5."

Check 7 (Factoring to Find Multiplicity). Factor f(x) = x³ − 6x² + 9xf of x equals x cubed minus 6 x squared plus 9 x. What are its zeros and their multiplicities?
Show answer

x = 0 (multiplicity 1) and x = 3 (multiplicity 2)

Where this is in your notes: item 3 and its example on pages 1 and 2 of the notes.

Check 8 (Classroom Example 3.2.6). Find the zeros of f(x) = x³ − 2x² − 9x + 18f of x equals x cubed minus 2 x squared minus 9 x plus 18. What does the graph do at each?
Show answer

Zeros 2, 3, and −3. It crosses at each.

Where this is in your notes: item 3 on pages 1 and 2, Classroom Example 3.2.6 on page 4 of the notes and the video "Classroom Example 3.2.6."
