2. Signed numbers

What you will be able to do

  • Add and subtract with negatives by thinking about direction.
  • Rewrite subtraction as adding the opposite.
  • Choose the sign of a product or quotient.

What a negative number is

Picture a number line. Zero sits in the middle. Positive numbers go to the right, and negative numbers go to the left. The number -3 negative 3 sits three steps to the left of zero. The minus sign in front tells you which side of zero the number is on.

Two numbers that sit the same distance from zero on opposite sides are called opposites. The opposite of 3 is -3 negative 3 , and the opposite of -3 negative 3 is 3. Opposites always add to zero: 3 + (-3) = 0 3 plus open parenthesis negative 3 close parenthesis equals 0 . Think of walking 3 steps right and then 3 steps left. You end where you started.

Adding: think of moving

To add, start at the first number and move. Adding a positive number moves you right. Adding a negative number moves you left.

2 + (-5): start at 2, move 5 left, land on -3 2 plus open parenthesis negative 5 close parenthesis : start at 2, move 5 left, land on negative 3 -4 + 6: start at -4, move 6 right, land on 2 negative 4 plus 6 : start at negative 4, move 6 right, land on 2 -1 + (-3): start at -1, move 3 left, land on -4 negative 1 plus open parenthesis negative 3 close parenthesis : start at negative 1, move 3 left, land on negative 4

A quick way that matches the number line: if the signs are the same, add the sizes and keep that sign. If the signs are different, subtract the smaller size from the larger size and keep the sign of the number with the larger size.

Subtracting: add the opposite

Subtracting a number does the same thing as adding its opposite. Subtracting 5 moves you 5 left, and so does adding -5 negative 5 . So every subtraction can be rewritten as an addition, and then you only need the adding rules.

7 - 10 = 7 + (-10) = -3 7 minus 10 equals 7 plus open parenthesis negative 10 close parenthesis equals negative 3 -2 - 6 = -2 + (-6) = -8 negative 2 minus 6 equals negative 2 plus open parenthesis negative 6 close parenthesis equals negative 8
Why this works. Subtracting a negative is the case that surprises people. Taking away a debt of 5 dollars leaves you 5 dollars better off. On the number line, subtracting means "move the opposite way from adding." Adding -5 negative 5 moves left, so subtracting -5 negative 5 moves right. That is why 3 - (-5) = 3 + 5 = 8 3 minus open parenthesis negative 5 close parenthesis equals 3 plus 5 equals 8 . Two minus signs side by side turn into a plus.

3. Substituting and simplifying

What you will be able to do

  • Replace x with a number, using parentheses.
  • Follow the order of operations.
  • Square negative numbers correctly.
  • Evaluate absolute value.

What substituting means

An equation like y = 2x + 7 y equals 2 x plus 7 is a rule. It says: take a number for x, multiply it by 2, then add 7, and the result is y. Substituting means replacing the letter with a specific number and then doing the arithmetic.

Two reminders about notation. A number written next to a letter means multiply, so 2x means 2 times x. And when you replace x with a number, put the number in parentheses. This matters most for negatives: with x = -3 x equals negative 3 , write 2(-3) 2 open parenthesis negative 3 close parenthesis , not 2-3 2 minus 3 , which would look like a subtraction.

The order of operations

When an expression has several operations, everyone has to do them in the same order or people would get different answers. The agreed order is:

  1. Work inside grouping symbols first: parentheses and absolute value bars.
  2. Powers (exponents).
  3. Multiplication and division, left to right.
  4. Addition and subtraction, left to right.

Absolute value

The absolute value of a number is its distance from zero on the number line. Distance is never negative. 4. Fractions and decimals on a graph

What you will be able to do

  • Read a fraction as a division and find its decimal.
  • Place a fraction between two whole numbers.
  • Simplify a fraction.

A fraction is a division

The fraction 7/2 means 7 divided by 2. You can also read it as "7 pieces, each one half of a whole." Seven halves make three wholes (that uses six halves) with one half left over, so 7/2 = 3 7 over 2 equals 3 and a half = 3.5.

This matters for graphing because answers in this course are often fractions, and you need to know roughly where they sit. You do not need a calculator. Ask: how many times does the bottom number go into the top number, and what is left over?

Locate a fraction

Where does 11/4 sit on a number line?

  1. 11 divided by 4 is 2 with 3 left over4 goes into 11 two times (that is 8), and 3 is left.
  2. 11/4 = 2 and 3/4 = 2.75 11 over 4 equals 2 and 3 over 4 equals 2.75 The leftover 3 is still divided by 4. And 3/4 = 0.75 3 over 4 equals 0.75 .
  3. between 2 and 3It sits three quarters of the way from 2 to 3.

11/4 is between 2 and 3, closer to 3.

Simplifying a fraction

Two fractions can name the same position. 2/4 and 1/2 are the same point on the number line. A fraction is simplified (or reduced) when no whole number other than 1 divides both the top and the bottom. To simplify, divide the top and the bottom by the same number.

8/12: both divide by 4, giving 2/3 8 over 12 : both divide by 4, giving 2 over 3 15/6: both divide by 3, giving 5/2 15 over 6 : both divide by 3, giving 5 over 2
Why this works. Dividing the top and the bottom by the same number is the same as dividing the fraction by 1, because 4/4 = 1 4 over 4 equals 1 . Dividing by 1 does not change a value. It only changes how the value is written.

In this course a fraction like 5/2 is a finished answer. You do not need to turn it into a mixed number, and MyMathLab expects the fraction form.

5. Solving for x to find intercepts

What you will be able to do

  • Explain why doing the same thing to both sides keeps an equation true.
  • Solve one-step and two-step equations.
  • Leave an answer as an exact fraction.

What solving means

An equation says two things are equal. Solving for x means finding the number that makes the statement true. For x + 4 = 10 x plus 4 equals 10 , the answer is 6 because 6 + 4 = 10 6 plus 4 equals 10 is a true statement.

Guessing works for small equations, but we want a method that always works. The method is to undo what was done to x, one step at a time, until x is alone.

The balance idea

Think of an equation as a scale that is balanced. If you do the same thing to both sides, it stays balanced. Add the same number to both sides, subtract the same number from both sides, multiply or divide both sides by the same number (not zero). Each of these keeps the equation true.

Each operation has an opposite that undoes it. Addition undoes subtraction. Division undoes multiplication.

How to undo each operation
If x has this done to itDo this to both sides
a number added (x + 5) open parenthesis x plus 5 close parenthesis subtract that number
a number subtracted (x - 5) open parenthesis x minus 5 close parenthesis add that number
multiplied by a number (5x)divide by that number

Solve 4x = 20 4 x equals 20 .

  2. 4x / 4 = 20 / 4 4 x over 4 equals 20 over 4 Divide both sides by 4 to undo the multiplication.
  3. x = 5 x equals 5 Check: 4(5) = 20 4 open parenthesis 5 close parenthesis equals 20 . True.

Two-step equations

When x has two things done to it, undo them in reverse order. In 2x + 6 2 x plus 6 , x was multiplied by 2 first and then 6 was added. So undo the adding first, then undo the multiplying. It is like taking off shoes before socks: the last thing put on comes off first.

A two-step equation with 0 on one side

Solve 0 = 4x - 3 0 equals 4 x minus 3 .

  1. 0 + 3 = 4x - 3 + 3 0 plus 3 equals 4 x minus 3 plus 3 Add 3 to both sides to undo the "minus 3".
  2. 3 = 4x 3 equals 4 x The x-term is now alone on its side.
  3. 3/4 = x 3 over 4 equals x Divide both sides by 4. Since 4 does not go into 3 evenly, leave the answer as a fraction.

Answer: x = 3/4. Check: 4(3/4) - 3 = 3 - 3 = 0 4 open parenthesis 3 over 4 close parenthesis minus 3 equals 3 minus 3 equals 0 . True.

It does not matter that x ended up on the right. 3/4 = x 3 over 4 equals x and x = 3/4 x equals 3 over 4 say the same thing. And a fraction answer is completely normal. Many answers in this course are fractions.

6. Points, quadrants, and entering them

What you will be able to do

  • Read an ordered pair as two moves from the origin.
  • Name the quadrant of a point from its signs.
  • Turn a substitution into a point.

The coordinate plane

Take two number lines and cross them at zero. The horizontal one is the x-axis. The vertical one is the y-axis. The point where they cross is the origin. Together they form a grid on which every location has an address.

The address is an ordered pair (x, y). The first number says how far to move left or right from the origin. The second number says how far to move up or down. Order matters: (2, 5) and (5, 2) are different places.

  • x positive: move right. x negative: move left.
  • y positive: move up. y negative: move down.

Plot a point

Describe how to plot (-4, 3) open parenthesis negative 4, 3 close parenthesis .

  1. start at the origin (0, 0)Every point is plotted from the origin.
  2. x = -4: move 4 left x equals negative 4 : move 4 left The first number is negative, so go left.
  3. y = 3: move 3 up y equals 3 : move 3 up The second number is positive, so go up.

The point is 4 left and 3 up from the origin.

Quadrants

The axes cut the plane into four regions called quadrants. They are numbered with Roman numerals, starting in the upper right and going counterclockwise.
