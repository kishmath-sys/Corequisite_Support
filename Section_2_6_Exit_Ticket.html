Where this is in your notes: item 1, Domain of a Polynomial Function, on page 1 of the notes.

Check 2 (Domain of a Rational Function). What is the domain of f(x) = 3x/(x + 5)f of x equals 3 x over the quantity x plus 5?
Show answer

(−∞, −5) ∪ (−5, ∞)all real numbers except negative 5

Where this is in your notes: item 1, Domain of a Rational Function, and its first example on page 2 of the notes.

Show answer

f(g(x)), with g placed inside f
