2. Reading the slope from an equation

What you will be able to do

  • Read slope and y-intercept from y = mx + b.
  • Handle a missing number, a reversed order, and x over a number.
  • Solve for y before reading the slope.

The pattern y = mx + b

Section 2.3 showed that when a line's equation is solved for y, it has the pattern y = mx + b. The number multiplied by x is the slope. The number standing alone is the y-intercept, the height where the line crosses the y-axis. Section 2.4 begins every problem by reading the slope out of an equation like this, so the reading has to be automatic.

y = 2x + 7 slope 2, y-intercept 7 y equals 2 x plus 7 slope 2, y minus intercept 7 y = (3/4)x - 1 slope 3/4, y-intercept -1 y equals open parenthesis 3 over 4 close parenthesis x minus 1 slope 3 over 4, y minus intercept negative 1

The sign in front of a number belongs to it. In the second line the y-intercept is -1 negative 1 , not 1.

Four cases that hide the numbers

  • No number in front of x. y = x + 4 y equals x plus 4 has slope 1, because x means 1x. And y = -x + 4 y equals negative x plus 4 has slope -1 negative 1 .
  • No number standing alone. y = 5x y equals 5 x has y-intercept 0. You can think of it as y = 5x + 0 y equals 5 x plus 0 .
  • Terms in the other order. y = 6 - 2x y equals 6 minus 2 x is the same as y = -2x + 6 y equals negative 2 x plus 6 . The slope is the number with the x, which is -2 negative 2 . It is not the number written first.
  • x over a number. y = x/2 is the same as y = (1/2)x y equals open parenthesis 1 over 2 close parenthesis x . The slope is 1/2.
Why m is the slope and b is the intercept. Put x = 0 x equals 0 into y = mx + b. The mx term becomes 0 and you get y = b y equals b . So the line passes through (0, b), which is on the y-axis. Now raise x by 1. The output goes up by exactly m. One step right, m steps up: that is rise over run with a run of 1, so m is the slope. Try it with y = 2x + 7 y equals 2 x plus 7 : at x = 0 x equals 0 , y = 7 y equals 7 . At x = 1 x equals 1 , y = 9 y equals 9 . Up 2 for 1 to the right.

If the equation is not solved for y

You can only read the slope when y is alone. If the equation is something like 2x + y = 9 2 x plus y equals 9 , solve for y first: y = -2x + 9 y equals negative 2 x plus 9 . Then the slope is -2 negative 2 . Reading the 2 straight from the original equation is a common slip.

Solve, then read

Find the slope of the line 6x + 2y = 10 6 x plus 2 y equals 10 .

  1. 2y = -6x + 10 2 y equals negative 6 x plus 10 Subtract 6x from both sides.
  2. y = -3x + 5 y equals negative 3 x plus 5 Divide every term by 2.
  3. slope = -3 slope equals negative 3 The number multiplied by x.

Answer: the slope is -3 negative 3 .

3. Opposites and reciprocals

What you will be able to do

  • Tell opposite from reciprocal.
  • Find the opposite reciprocal of a whole number or fraction.
  • Check by multiplying to get -1 negative 1 .

Two different ways to change a number

The lecture uses the phrase "opposite reciprocal." It is two separate changes done to one number. Each is simple. The trouble comes from mixing them up or doing only one.

Opposite: change the sign

The opposite of a number has the same size and the other sign. The opposite of 4 is -4 negative 4 . The opposite of -2/3 negative 2 over 3 is 2/3. A number and its opposite add to zero.

Reciprocal: flip the fraction

The reciprocal of a fraction swaps its top and bottom. The reciprocal of 2/7 is 7/2. The sign does not change when you flip: the reciprocal of -1/4 negative 1 over 4 is -4/1 negative 4 over 1 , which is -4 negative 4 .

A whole number is a fraction with 1 on the bottom. Write 5 as 5/1. Flip it and you get 1/5. So the reciprocal of 5 is 1/5.

What a reciprocal really is. A number times its reciprocal is always 1. (2/7)(7/2) = 14/14 = 1 open parenthesis 2 over 7 close parenthesis open parenthesis 7 over 2 close parenthesis equals 14 over 14 equals 1 . To multiply fractions, multiply the tops and multiply the bottoms. Flipping makes the top of one match the bottom of the other, so everything cancels.

Opposite reciprocal: do both

Flip the fraction and change the sign. The order does not matter.

2 becomes 1/2 becomes -1/2 2 becomes 1 over 2 becomes negative 1 over 2 -3/5 becomes -5/3 becomes 5/3 negative 3 over 5 becomes negative 5 over 3 becomes 5 over 3 1/6 becomes 6 becomes -6 1 over 6 becomes 6 becomes negative 6

Notice the middle line. The starting number was negative, so its opposite reciprocal is positive. "Opposite" does not mean "make it negative." It means "change the sign," whatever the sign was.

A check you can always run

A number times its opposite reciprocal is always -1 negative 1 . If your product is not -1 negative 1 , something went wrong.

(2)(-1/2) = -2/2 = -1 open parenthesis 2 close parenthesis open parenthesis negative 1 over 2 close parenthesis equals negative 2 over 2 equals negative 1 (-3/5)(5/3) = -15/15 = -1 open parenthesis negative 3 over 5 close parenthesis open parenthesis 5 over 3 close parenthesis equals negative 15 over 15 equals negative 1

Find and check

Find the opposite reciprocal of 4/9 and check it.

  1. 9/4 9 over 4 Flip the fraction.
  2. -9/4 negative 9 over 4 Change the sign. It was positive, so now it is negative.
  3. (4/9)(-9/4) = -36/36 = -1 open parenthesis 4 over 9 close parenthesis open parenthesis negative 9 over 4 close parenthesis equals negative 36 over 36 equals negative 1 The check works.

Answer: -9/4 negative 9 over 4

4. Filling in y - y1 = m(x - x1) y minus y 1 equals m open parenthesis x minus x 1 close parenthesis

What you will be able to do

  • Put a point and a slope into the form without swapping coordinates.
  • Clear double negatives.
  • Distribute and solve for y.

The pattern, one more time

Section 2.3 introduced point-slope form: y - y1 = m(x - x1) y minus y 1 equals m open parenthesis x minus x 1 close parenthesis . Here (x1, y1) is a point the line passes through and m is the slope. Section 2.4 uses this form in every example and does not re-teach it. So this section practices only the filling in and the cleaning up.

Filling in

Three numbers go in. The x-coordinate of the point replaces x1. The y-coordinate replaces y1. The slope replaces m. The plain x and y stay as letters. Put each number in parentheses first, then clean up.

point (2, -6), slope 4 point open parenthesis 2, negative 6 close parenthesis, slope 4 y - (-6) = 4(x - (2)) y minus open parenthesis negative 6 close parenthesis equals 4 open parenthesis x minus open parenthesis 2 close parenthesis close parenthesis y + 6 = 4(x - 2) y plus 6 equals 4 open parenthesis x minus 2 close parenthesis

Two things to watch. First, x1 goes with x and y1 goes with y. It is easy to swap them because the y part is written first in the form but second in the point. Second, a negative coordinate turns the minus in the form into a plus.

Where the form comes from. The form is the slope formula in disguise. Slope is (change in y) over (change in x). Take any point (x, y) on the line and the known point (x1, y1): the slope between them is (y - y1 y minus y 1 )/(x - x1 x minus x 1 ) = m. Multiply both sides by (x - x1) open parenthesis x minus x 1 close parenthesis and you have y - y1 = m(x - x1) y minus y 1 equals m open parenthesis x minus x 1 close parenthesis . The two minus signs are there because slope is built from subtractions.

Cleaning up to y = mx + b

From here the steps are the ones practiced on the Section 2.3 page: distribute the slope, then add or subtract to leave y alone.

Fill in and clean up

A line has slope 2/5 and passes through (-5, 3) open parenthesis negative 5, 3 close parenthesis . Write it in point-slope form, then solve for y.

  1. y - (3) = (2/5)(x - (-5)) y minus open parenthesis 3 close parenthesis equals open parenthesis 2 over 5 close parenthesis open parenthesis x minus open parenthesis negative 5 close parenthesis close parenthesis Fill in with parentheses.
  2. y - 3 = (2/5)(x + 5) y minus 3 equals open parenthesis 2 over 5 close parenthesis open parenthesis x plus 5 close parenthesis Clear the double negative. This is point-slope form.
  3. y - 3 = (2/5)x + 2 y minus 3 equals open parenthesis 2 over 5 close parenthesis x plus 2 Distribute. Two fifths of 5 is 2.
  4. y = (2/5)x + 5 y equals open parenthesis 2 over 5 close parenthesis x plus 5 Add 3 to both sides.

Answer: y - 3 = (2/5)(x + 5) y minus 3 equals open parenthesis 2 over 5 close parenthesis open parenthesis x plus 5 close parenthesis , and y = (2/5)x + 5 y equals open parenthesis 2 over 5 close parenthesis x plus 5 .

5. Adding a fraction and a whole number

What you will be able to do

  • Rewrite a whole number with a chosen denominator.
  • Add or subtract a fraction and a whole number.
  • Use the Desmos fraction button to check.

When the numbers do not come out even

Sometimes distributing a fraction slope leaves a fraction behind, such as -1/4. Then the last step, adding or subtracting a whole number, mixes a fraction with a whole number. This is the step the instructor calls annoying. It is one small skill.

Give the whole number the same bottom

Fractions can be added only when their bottoms match. The bottom is called the denominator. A whole number can be written with any denominator you like: multiply it by that denominator and put the result on top.

3 = 6/2 because 6 divided by 2 is 3 3 equals 6 over 2 because 6 divided by 2 is 3 2 = 8/4 because 8 divided by 4 is 2 2 equals 8 over 4 because 8 divided by 4 is 2 4 = 12/3 4 equals 12 over 3

Then add or subtract the tops and keep the bottom.

1/2 + 3 = 1/2 + 6/2 = 7/2 1 over 2 plus 3 equals 1 over 2 plus 6 over 2 equals 7 over 2 -1/4 - 2 = -1/4 - 8/4 = -9/4 negative 1 over 4 minus 2 equals negative 1 over 4 minus 8 over 4 equals negative 9 over 4
A picture that helps. Think of pizza cut into fourths. Two whole pizzas are 8 slices. If you owe 1 slice and then owe 2 more whole pizzas, you owe 9 slices in all: -9/4. The denominator names the size of the slice, and you can only count slices together when they are the same size.

Distributing when it does not divide evenly

If the bottom of the fraction does not divide the number, multiply across the top and leave the fraction.

(-2/3)(x + 2) = (-2/3)x - 4/3 because 2 * 2 = 4, over 3 open parenthesis negative 2 over 3 close parenthesis open parenthesis x plus 2 close parenthesis equals open parenthesis negative 2 over 3 close parenthesis x minus 4 over 3 because 2 times 2 equals 4, over 3

Desmos will do the arithmetic

The lecture uses Desmos for this step, and you may too. Type the calculation, for example -1/4 - 2 negative 1 over 4 minus 2 . Desmos shows a decimal, -2.25 negative 2.25 . Click the fraction button beside the answer and it shows -9/4. Knowing how to do it by hand lets you tell when a typed answer looks wrong.

The last step with fractions

Solve y + 2 = (-3/4)x - 1/4 y plus 2 equals open parenthesis negative 3 over 4 close parenthesis x minus 1 over 4 for y.

  1. y = (-3/4)x - 1/4 - 2 y equals open parenthesis negative 3 over 4 close parenthesis x minus 1 over 4 minus 2 Subtract 2 from both sides.
  2. y = (-3/4)x - 1/4 - 8/4 y equals open parenthesis negative 3 over 4 close parenthesis x minus 1 over 4 minus 8 over 4 Write 2 as 8/4.
  3. y = (-3/4)x - 9/4 y equals open parenthesis negative 3 over 4 close parenthesis x minus 9 over 4 Combine the tops: -1 - 8 = -9 negative 1 minus 8 equals negative 9 .

Answer: y = (-3/4)x - 9/4 y equals open parenthesis negative 3 over 4 close parenthesis x minus 9 over 4
