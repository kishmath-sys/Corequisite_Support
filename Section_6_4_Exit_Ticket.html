Check 1 (Multiplicative Inverse of a Number). What is the multiplicative inverse of 5? Enter it as a decimal.

Hint

Two numbers are multiplicative inverses if their product is 1.

Show answer

0.2

Where this is in your notes: item 1a (Multiplicative Inverse) on page 1 of the notes.

Check 2 (Inverse Matrices). When are square matrices A and B multiplicative inverses?
Show answer

When AB = BA = I, the identity matrix

Where this is in your notes: item 1a (Multiplicative Inverse) and its matrix example on page 1 of the notes.

Check 3 (Singular and Nonsingular). A matrix does not have an inverse. What is it called?
Show answer

Singular

Where this is in your notes: items 1b and 1c (Nonsingular and Singular Matrix) on page 1 of the notes.

Check 4 (Classroom Example 6.4.1). Find AB and BA for A = [2 1; 1 1]matrix with row 1: 2, 1; row 2: 1, 1 and B = [1 −1; −1 2]matrix with row 1: 1, negative 1; row 2: negative 1, 2. Is B the inverse of A?
Show answer

Yes, because AB and BA are both the identity matrix.

Where this is in your notes: item 1a on page 1, Classroom Example 6.4.1 on page 6 of the notes and the video "Classroom Example 6.4.1."

Check 5 (A Row of Zeros). While finding an inverse by hand, one row of the original matrix turns into all 0s. What does that mean?
Show answer

The matrix is singular and has no inverse.
