1. How this works

Why this is here. In a face-to-face class, your instructor, Matt Read, would see how the lesson went just by looking around the room. Online, this exit ticket does that job. It has 9 quick checks on the Section 2.4 notes and videos. It is ungraded, and nobody sees your answers unless you choose to send them.
  • Do this after you finish the Section 2.4 notes and videos. It takes about 10 to 15 minutes.
  • Each check tells you right away whether you have it. If not, it names the exact item, page, or video to go back to.
  • Section 4 shows your results and makes it easy to reach Matt by email or set up a quick Zoom meeting.
  • Your answers save on this device so you can come back later.
