A short, ungraded check after the Section 1.3 notes and videos. It shows what clicked, points you back to the right page or video for anything that did not, and makes it easy to reach Matt.


1. How this works

Why this is here. In a face-to-face class, your instructor, Matt Read, would see how the lesson went just by looking around the room. Online, this exit ticket does that job. It has 10 quick checks on the Section 1.3 notes and videos. It is ungraded, and nobody sees your answers unless you choose to send them.
  • Do this after you finish the Section 1.3 notes and videos. It takes about 10 to 15 minutes.
  • Each check tells you right away whether you have it. If not, it names the exact item, page, or video to go back to.
  • Section 4 shows your results and makes it easy to reach Matt by email or set up a quick Zoom meeting.
  • Your answers save on this device so you can come back later.

Check 6 (Perimeter Equation). A rectangular garden is 3 times as long as it is wide, and its perimeter is 64 meters. With w as the width, which equation fits?
Show answer

w + 3w + w + 3w = 64w plus 3 w plus w plus 3 w equals 64
